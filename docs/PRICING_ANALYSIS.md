# Project Pricing Analysis: Business AI Assistant

**Prepared For:** Senior Developer (Kenya)
**Target Market:** Toronto, Canada
**Project Type:** Full-stack SaaS with AI Integration
**Complexity:** Medium-High

---

## 1. Executive Summary

You have a significant **arbitrage opportunity**. As a Senior Developer in Kenya selling to the Toronto market, you can offer highly competitive rates (by Canadian standards) while earning premium rates (by local standards).

*   **Your Value:** Senior expertise, full-stack capability, AI specialization.
*   **The Market:** Toronto is a high-cost tech hub. Local agencies charge $150-$250/hr.
*   **Strategy:** "Premium Offshore." Price yourself above Upwork freelancers but significantly below Toronto agencies.

For a project of this size ($48k), **Cash Flow is critical**.

**Recommended: Milestone-Based**
1.  **Deposit (Kickoff):** 25% ($12,000) - *Required to start.*
2.  **Milestone 1 (Backend Core):** 25% ($12,000) - *After API & Auth complete.*
3.  **Milestone 2 (Frontend Beta):** 25% ($12,000) - *After Dashboard & Chat are usable.*
4.  **Final (Delivery):** 25% ($12,000) - *Upon deployment & handover.*
